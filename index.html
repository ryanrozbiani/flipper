How this works
  1. Paste a LIVE link (dynamic_playlist.m3u8?type=live), a MASTER link (master_playlist.m3u8) or a REPLAY link (playlist_N.m3u8).
  2. After a Space ends, X moves the recording from the live folder (x-media-live-stream-…) to a replay folder (periscope-replays-public or periscope-replay-direct-prod-…) and sometimes to the prod-fastly-p server. The old converter only looked in the live folder, which is why its links broke.
  3. This page tries every known location, follows the master playlist to the numbered playlist_N, and only shows a REPLAY link once it has actually loaded audio from it.
  4. A Space link (x.com/i/spaces/…) is sent to your Cloudflare Worker, which asks X for the Space's stream link. Then everything above runs as normal.
  5. Tip: you can link straight to a conversion: yourpage.html?link=https://x.com/i/spaces/…
Downloader
Downloads the REPLAY above (or the link you pasted) and saves it as one file: M4A (original quality) or MP3. If the Space is still live, it keeps recording until you press Stop.
